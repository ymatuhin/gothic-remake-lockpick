Gothic 1 Remake Lockpicking Guide and Chest Lock Solver

Players usually describe this mechanic as Gothic 1 Remake lockpicking, a lockpick minigame, a chest lock puzzle, or simply the Gothic lock solver problem. Some guides call the moving parts plates, while forum posts often say sliders, pins, tumblers, rows, or gates. This tool uses the in-game idea of moving plates, but it also works for anyone searching for a lockpicking solver, chest solver, slider puzzle solver, or lockbreaker.

Lockpicking in Gothic 1 Remake is a logic puzzle built around moving plates, not the old left-right combination from the original Gothic 1. Each chest lock has several plates with seven possible positions. To open the lock, every plate must finish in the center position, marked as position 4.

The hard part is that plates are linked. Moving one plate can also move other plates in the same direction or in the opposite direction, so a single move may improve one part of the lock while pushing another plate away from the center. This lockpick solver lets you copy the starting positions from the in-game lock, add the Same and Opposite links, and calculate a shortest sequence of moves.

The current lock configuration is saved in the page URL automatically, so you can bookmark a puzzle, refresh the page without losing it, or share the exact setup with someone else.

How to use the Gothic lockpick solver

  1. Select the number of plates shown on the chest lock.
  2. Mark the current position of each plate from 1 to 7.
  3. Enter linked plates in Same or Opposite when moving a plate affects another one.
  4. Press Solve and follow the compact plate-and-arrow solution.

Why lockpicking feels difficult in the remake

Gothic 1 Remake treats lockpicking as a readable mechanical system: observe what changes, map the links, then move the plates into the center without forcing an invalid edge move. Training lockpicking in the game can make the process more forgiving, but the puzzle itself still depends on understanding how the plates influence each other.
